Dental implants · Phoenix

Clearview built their whole consultation around the coverage.

Clearview were converting well once a patient sat down with them, and poorly before that. Full arch cases run into serious money, and prospects were shopping three or four practices before booking anything.

We built the release around their move to a same day arch protocol and the surgeon behind it, then syndicated it nationally. The links went onto their consultation page, into their follow up emails and into the folder handed to every prospect who walked in.

  • A press page that answers the trust question before it is asked
  • Coverage links used in every consultation follow up
  • Patients raising the coverage themselves in the room

Roofing · Dallas Fort Worth

Sumner Exteriors stopped sounding like every other roofer in the market.

After a bad hail season, every contractor in the metro was running the same ads with the same promises. Sumner had a genuinely better warranty and no way to make anyone believe it above the noise.

We ran their story as a homeowner warning piece: what to check after hail, what the common insurance traps are, and what a legitimate warranty actually covers. Useful to read, credible in print, and their name on all of it.

  • A news angle competitors could not simply copy into an ad
  • Coverage worked into their door and estimate follow up
  • A story worth sending to insurance adjusters and homeowners alike

Med spa · Tampa

Lumen Aesthetics announced a second location and got two things out of it.

Lumen were opening a second clinic and had budgeted for a launch campaign. What they did not have was any independent coverage, which mattered because the new area had no idea who they were.

We wrote the expansion as a business story: the growth behind it, the treatments coming to the area and the team being hired. Syndicated, it introduced them to a new market and gave their hiring ads something credible to point at.

  • A launch backed by coverage instead of ads alone
  • Recognition in a market that had never heard the name
  • Hiring conversations that started warmer
