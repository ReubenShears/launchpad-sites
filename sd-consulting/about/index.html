Our story

Started because good operators kept losing to louder ones.

SD Consulting grew out of years spent inside sales and marketing for high ticket services. The pattern was always the same. The clinic with the better surgeon lost the case to the one with the bigger name. The contractor with the twenty year record lost the remodel to the outfit with a press page and a few logos. The work was not the difference. The perception was.

Traditional PR was not built for these businesses. Retainers running into thousands a month, six month commitments and a lot of talk about relationships, aimed at brands with a communications department. A dental practice or a roofing company does not need any of that. It needs a credible, well written story out in the world that a prospect can find on their own.

So we stripped it back to the piece that actually moves a sale. One flat fee, one properly written release, syndicated nationally, with the live links handed straight back to you. No retainer, no lock in, and no invoice arriving before you have seen anything for it.
