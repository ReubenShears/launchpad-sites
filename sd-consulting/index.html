Questions

The things owners ask before their first release.

What exactly does the $950 cover?

The angle call, the fully written release, your revisions, distribution across the syndication network and the final report of live links. There is no separate writing fee, no monthly retainer and nothing added afterward.

What if my business has no big news?

Almost every business we speak to thinks this, and almost every one is wrong. A new location, a new piece of equipment, a hiring push, a seasonal warning for homeowners or a patient milestone are all legitimate news angles. Finding the story is our job, not yours.

How long does it take?

The angle call happens within a few days of you starting. Your draft follows shortly after, and distribution goes out once you approve it. Most clients see their coverage links inside the same couple of weeks.

Do you only work with medical and home improvement businesses?

Those are the two markets we know deeply, and they are where our writing is strongest. We take the occasional high ticket business outside them, but only when we are confident we can do the story justice.

Can I use the coverage in my own advertising?

Yes. The links are yours to use on your website, in your ads, on your consultation follow ups and in your sales conversations. We will show you the placements that tend to move the needle most.
