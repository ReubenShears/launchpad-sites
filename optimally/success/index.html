Joinery & fitting

Two years of putting it off, sorted in a week.

James ran a busy fitting and joinery outfit almost entirely on referrals. He had been quoted several thousand pounds twice and walked away both times, so the business had no website at all and the only way to reach him was a mobile number on a van.

  • Wrote the offer around his highest value work rather than listing every job he could do
  • Built and launched a three page site inside a week, with the enquiry form going to his phone
  • Enquiries now arrive with the detail he needs to quote, instead of a voicemail he returns at nine at night

Coaching

The leads were fine. The follow up was the leak.

A one to one coach was spending steadily on social content and getting a decent number of enquiries, but replying in batches whenever she found a gap between sessions. By then, most people had already booked elsewhere or gone cold.

  • Rebuilt the landing page so the offer and the price bracket were clear before anyone enquired
  • Wired the form to send an instant reply plus a text alert, with a nudge if nothing was answered
  • Fewer, better conversations, and no more discovery calls with people who were never going to buy
1 page
Replaced a site nobody could navigate
Clinic

Sending paid traffic to a page that finally matched the ad.

A private clinic was running ads to a homepage built around a menu of fourteen treatments. Visitors landed, hunted for the thing the ad had promised, and left. The clinic assumed the ads were the problem.

  • Built a dedicated page for the single treatment the ads were promoting, with pricing stated plainly
  • Booking enquiries captured on the page itself rather than a phone number in the footer
  • The same ad budget now produces enquiries the front desk can actually work with
