Roofing · Harrogate

Mercer Roofing went from a dead February to booked out to Christmas.

David had been through two agencies in three years. Both reported healthy click numbers, neither could tell him how many roofs those clicks sold. Winter was consistently dead and he was carrying a four-man team through it.

We rebuilt the account around full re-roofs and storm damage work, blocked the repair-quote searches eating a third of the budget, and put call tracking on every route in.

  • Enquiries up 214% within the first quarter
  • Cost per enquiry down from £38 to £12.60
  • First full winter without a quiet fortnight

Electrical · Leeds

Kirkgate Electrical stopped quoting jobs they didn't want.

Kirkgate were getting plenty of calls, but most were for single sockets and fault-finding: an hour of travel for forty quid of work. The commercial contracts they actually wanted were nowhere to be seen.

We split the account by job value, pushed budget behind consumer unit upgrades, rewires and commercial maintenance, and wrote ad copy that quietly filtered the small stuff out.

  • £186,000 of tracked work in the first twelve months
  • Average job value up from £220 to £1,340
  • Two ongoing commercial maintenance contracts won

Cost per booked job
Plumbing & heating · York

Ashfield Heating halved their cost per job without spending a penny more.

Ashfield's budget wasn't the problem, the targeting was. They were bidding on boiler repair searches across a forty mile radius, including areas their engineers couldn't reach profitably.

We tightened the radius to where the vans actually are, moved spend to boiler installation and heat pump enquiries, and rebuilt the landing page around finance options.

  • Cost per booked job down 61% on the same monthly spend
  • Installation enquiries now 70% of the mix, up from 25%
  • Third engineer hired eight months in
