About us

Built to compound, not to impress.

Legazy Family Marketing is a marketing consultancy for service based businesses and entrepreneurs who are tired of tactics and want a system. We build the engine, connect it, automate it and keep it improving.

Our story

The businesses that win are the ones with a system behind them.

Legazy Family Marketing started with a pattern that kept repeating. Good operators with genuinely good offers were spending real money on marketing and still could not answer a simple question: where does the next customer come from, and what did the last one cost? They had ads running, a funnel half built, a mailing list nobody emailed and a CRM full of leads that went quiet. Every piece existed. Nothing was connected.

So we stopped selling pieces. Instead of another campaign or another landing page, we build one four step system: Attract, Convert, Automate, Optimize. Paid acquisition brings the right people in. Funnels and lifecycle messaging turn them into booked customers. AI powered workflows qualify and follow up around the clock, and weekly optimization keeps cost per acquisition heading down while revenue heads up. Each stage feeds the next, which is why the results tend to build rather than plateau.

The name is deliberate. Legacy is what gets built when growth stops depending on one person being online at the right moment, and family is how we work with the businesses we take on. We stay small on purpose, take on clients we can genuinely move, and treat every account as if the numbers were our own. If a channel is not working, we say so. If a budget is not ready yet, we say that too.
