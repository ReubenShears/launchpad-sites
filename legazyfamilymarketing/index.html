Questions

The things people ask before they start.

What kind of businesses do you work with?

Service based businesses, coaches, consultants and agencies that sell a considered offer and are ready to scale predictably. If your customers make a decision worth more than a few hundred dollars, the system fits.

How much should I be spending on ads?

That depends on your market and your offer, and we will tell you straight on the discovery call if your budget is not ready to move the needle yet. Management is quoted separately from media spend, so you always know which is which.

What does the AI automation actually do?

It answers, qualifies and follows up with every new lead the moment it arrives, day or night, then hands you the ones worth a conversation. It also keeps your CRM tidy and your reporting current without anybody doing data entry.

Am I locked into a long contract?

No. Engagements run month to month once the build is complete. The system is designed to earn its place, and if it is not producing you should be free to walk.

Who owns the accounts and the data?

You do, always. Ad accounts, CRM, automations and creative are built in your name, so if we ever part ways the system, the history and the data stay with you.
