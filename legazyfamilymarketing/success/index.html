Home services

A busy operator with ad spend and no pipeline to show for it

The ads were running and the budget was leaving the account every month, but enquiries landed in a shared inbox and were answered whenever somebody had a spare hour. Nobody could say which campaign produced a job.

What we did: rebuilt the funnel around the highest value service, moved every enquiry into a single tracked pipeline, and put an AI agent on first response so leads were answered in minutes rather than days.

  • Booked calls doubled inside the first 60 days
  • Every booking traceable back to the campaign that produced it
  • First response time cut from days to minutes, including weekends

Coaching practice

A founder doing the qualifying, the chasing and the delivery

Enquiries came in steadily, but every one of them needed the founder to qualify it, book it and follow up on it. Delivery time was being eaten by admin, and the good leads were queued behind the ones going nowhere.

What we did: built a qualification flow that asks the right questions before anything reaches a human, connected it to the calendar, and added lifecycle email and SMS so nobody goes quiet by accident.

  • Around ten hours a week returned to client delivery
  • Every lead qualified before it reaches the inbox
  • Follow up runs on its own, including the slow leads that convert later

Lower cost per acquisition
B2B services

Plenty of reporting, none of it useful for making decisions

Spend was spread thin across channels with a dashboard for each, and no view of what a customer actually cost. Creative was refreshed when someone remembered, which meant performance drifted quietly between reviews.

What we did: consolidated tracking into one source of truth, cut the channels that could not be justified, and put creative on a weekly test cycle with a plain English report against cost per acquisition.

  • Cost per acquisition down by 38 percent within a quarter
  • Budget consolidated into the channels that could be proven
  • One weekly report the leadership team can actually make decisions from
