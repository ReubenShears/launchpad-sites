Questions

The things teams ask before they switch.

What if the person I am messaging is not on iMessage?

Sendblue detects it automatically and falls back to RCS, then to SMS, so the message still lands. You do not have to maintain separate lists or separate logic for Android contacts.

Do I need A2P registration to get started?

No. That is one of the main reasons teams move over. You skip the two to three week registration process entirely and can start messaging the same day you sign up.

What does it cost?

There is a free sandbox for testing with full API access, then flat rate pricing per line for production, starting at $100 per line per month. No per message fees and no hidden carrier charges. High volume and outbound teams move to a custom Enterprise plan.

Can I bring my own number?

Yes. Existing numbers can be ported onto the platform, and you can also provision new dedicated lines per rep or per workflow, which is how most sales teams set it up.

Is Sendblue secure enough for regulated industries?

Sendblue is SOC 2 Type II certified with end to end encryption, and HIPAA plans with enterprise BAAs are available. Healthcare, financial services and other regulated teams run on the platform today.
