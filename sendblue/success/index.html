Outbound sales · B2B fintech

A 40 rep sales floor stopped being ignored.

The team was running a well built SMS sequence out of HubSpot and getting the response rate everyone in their category gets, which is to say almost none. Prospects were not offended by the outreach, they simply never opened it.

They moved one sequence to Sendblue as a controlled test, gave each rep a dedicated line, and kept the copy identical so the only variable was the channel. The comparison was not close, and the rest of the floor migrated within the month.

  • Reply rate roughly tripled against the SMS control
  • Every rep on their own line, all threads visible in the shared inbox
  • Conversations logged back to HubSpot with no extra rep effort
3 weeks
Saved by skipping A2P registration
AI agents · Developer platform

A messaging agent shipped in an afternoon instead of a quarter.

The product was an assistant that books travel and handles inboxes on behalf of its users. The hard part was never the model, it was the channel: carrier registration, message segments, and a delivery pipeline the team would have to babysit forever.

They provisioned a number, installed the SDK and had the agent replying over iMessage the same day, with webhooks handling inbound turns and delivery receipts feeding straight back into their own event log.

  • Live on a real number in under a minute, no registration queue
  • Two way conversations handled through webhooks out of the box
  • Automatic RCS and SMS fallback for users not on iMessage
One bill
Flat rate per line, no message fees
Client communications · Brokerage

A brokerage swapped an unpredictable bill for a fixed one.

Per message pricing meant their busiest months, the ones where client communication mattered most, produced the largest and least explainable invoices. Finance had stopped trusting the forecast for the line item entirely.

Moving to flat rate lines made the cost a function of headcount rather than activity, and the shift to iMessage meant clients started replying to the updates instead of calling the office to ask about them.

  • Messaging spend now forecastable to the dollar
  • Voice memos and full quality documents sent in thread
  • Inbound call volume down as clients replied in the thread instead
