Our story

It started with a simple, annoying observation.

People answer their friends. They ignore their vendors. The difference is rarely the message, it is the channel it arrives on. A blue bubble reads as a person, a green one reads as a broadcast, and every revenue team in the world was stuck sending the second kind while quietly wishing they could send the first.

The workarounds were ugly. Racks of Mac minis in closets, brittle scripts, accounts that died the moment Apple shipped an update. Anyone who tried to build on iMessage seriously learned the same lesson: making it work once is easy, making it work every time for thousands of businesses is a genuine infrastructure problem.

So that is what we built. Sendblue is the boring, dependable layer underneath the interesting part: native iMessage delivery with automatic RCS and SMS fallback, webhooks that fire on every reply, and a CRM integration your reps do not have to think about. Five years, tens of thousands of businesses, and a lot of messages later, it is the part of the stack our customers stop worrying about.
