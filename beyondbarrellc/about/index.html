Our story

Built by a clinician who spent her life in both rooms.

Dr. Jessica Aquino has spent her career on both sides of the studio door: in the clinic as a physical therapist and athletic trainer, and in the performing arts world where the injuries she treats are actually produced. That combination is unusual, and it is the whole reason Beyond BARRE exists.

The pattern she kept seeing was always the same. A dancer would come in after months of being told to rest, having been assessed lying on a table by someone who had never seen a plié, a relevé or a landing. The symptoms would settle, the dancer would return to class, and within a fortnight everything would come back, because nothing about how they moved had changed.

So the practice was built around the opposite approach. Sessions are one to one and long enough to watch technique properly. Assessment covers biomechanics, strength, recovery capacity and training load, not just the sore joint. And the goal is never simply the absence of pain: it is a body that can hold up to the choreography being asked of it, season after season.

How we work

Three things this practice will not compromise on.

◈

You see the doctor, every time

Sessions are one to one with Dr. Jessica from the first evaluation to the last. Nothing is handed off to an aide halfway through, and nobody is sent to a corner with a sheet of exercises.

◎

We assess the art form, not just the joint

Turnout, alignment, landing mechanics, recovery time and rehearsal load all get looked at, because the injury sitting in front of us was produced by all of them together.

◐

Honest timelines, honest answers

If something needs longer than you want to hear, you will hear it. If this is not the right practice for what you are dealing with, you will be told that on the free intro call.
