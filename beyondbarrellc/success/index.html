Ballet · Pre-professional

Maya stopped losing every season to the same hip.

Maya had been managing hip pain for two years. Every time it settled she returned to full class, and within a fortnight it was back. Two previous clinicians had assessed her lying on a table and told her to rest.

The evaluation looked at her turnout, her pelvic control and how she was compensating through her lower back during développé. The plan kept her in modified class throughout while we rebuilt deep hip control and load tolerance, then staged her back up in line with her rehearsal calendar.

  • Stayed in modified class rather than taking the season off
  • Danced her spring performance without symptoms
  • Now has a maintenance routine she runs herself
Full run
Eight shows a week, completed
Musical theater · Working performer

Daniel got through a full run without his ankle giving out.

Daniel was three weeks from opening with an ankle that had never fully recovered from a landing the previous year. He could get through a rehearsal, but not through eight shows a week, and taping was doing the work his ankle should have been doing.

We assessed his landing mechanics and calf capacity, found how little load the ankle was actually tolerating, and built a short daily program around the show schedule with recovery blocks written into the week rather than hoped for.

  • Completed the full run without missing a performance
  • Came off dependence on taping to get through a show
  • Left with a warm-up and recovery routine he still uses

Competitive dance · Teen athlete

Sofia made it through competition season without a break.

Sofia's knee pain arrived in the middle of competition season, the worst possible timing. Her family had been told the options were to stop for six weeks or push through it, and neither answer worked for a season already booked.

The screening showed the knee was carrying load that her hips and ankles were not sharing. We adjusted her training volume, rebuilt the strength either side of the joint, and worked with her family so everyone understood the timeline and the limits.

  • Competed the full season with a managed training load
  • Knee pain resolved without stopping training entirely
  • Parents left understanding the plan, not just following it
