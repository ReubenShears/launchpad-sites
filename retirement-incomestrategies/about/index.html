About us

Integrity and trustworthiness are the foundation of this firm.

Retirement Income Planners was built on one idea: retirement concepts are made far more confusing than they need to be, and people make better decisions when somebody takes the time to explain them properly.

AH
Allen Horton, Certified Annuity Specialist

President, Retirement Income Planners

Allen has a passion for making confusing retirement concepts simple and easy to understand. The work starts by identifying your specific retirement income needs and concerns. Only then do we educate you on how annuities work and whether they can address those concerns. If they cannot, you will be told so plainly.

Because the firm is independent rather than tied to a few insurance carriers, we can search the available carriers and their fixed index annuity contracts to find the strongest options for your particular need. Allen is a member of the Million Dollar Round Table, the international association representing leading professionals in the life insurance based financial services industry, and of NAFA, the National Association for Fixed Annuities, where he keeps current with the policies and trends that affect the contracts his clients hold.

Alongside his work with families, Allen continues to serve his country. He is a field grade commissioned officer, a Major in the U.S. Army Reserves, and has served for over thirty years, including a year long tour in Iraq and Kuwait where he earned numerous awards for his performance. He has been married for twenty eight years, is the father of three children, and lives in Brentwood, Tennessee.

"People do not need more products explained at them. They need somebody to sit down, work out what they actually need, and tell them the truth about whether it can be done."

How we work

Three principles that have not changed since day one.

◈

Your needs come before any product

We identify what your retirement actually requires before a single contract is discussed. If what you need is not what we do, we will say so and point you toward whoever can help.

⛨

Independent, so the whole market is open

We are not tied to a handful of carriers. That means we compare contracts across the market on your behalf, and it is why our recommendations differ so much from one client to the next.

☰

Plain English, always

You should be able to explain your own retirement plan to your children in a couple of sentences. If you cannot, we have not finished doing our job properly.
