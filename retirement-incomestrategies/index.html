Questions

The things people ask before they sit down with us.

What does the first conversation cost?

Nothing. The first conversation is a chance for us to understand your situation and for you to decide whether we are the right people to help. There is no fee and no obligation attached to it.

Do I have to move all my money?

No. Most plans we write use part of a client's savings to create protected lifetime income, leaving the rest invested for growth and access. How much sits in each bucket is your decision, made once you can see the numbers.

Are you tied to one insurance company?

We are independent. That means we can search the available carriers and their fixed index annuity contracts rather than recommending from a short list, and it is why two clients with similar balances often end up with very different plans.

What if annuities are not right for me?

We will tell you. Our job is to identify your specific needs and concerns first, then show you honestly whether these products address them. If they do not, you will hear that from us rather than a sales pitch.

What about long term care costs?

Extended care costs have risen substantially over the last ten years and belong in every retirement plan. We work with hybrid plans that leverage your dollars if care is ever needed and remain accessible to you if it is not.
