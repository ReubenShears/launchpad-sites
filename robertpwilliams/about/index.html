The story

Built from the same problem you have.

I started out on the delivery side of service work, and I learned the hard way what most owners eventually find out: being genuinely good at the work is not a growth strategy. The projects were strong, the clients were happy, and the pipeline still went quiet every time I put my head down for a month.

So I went and fixed that properly for myself first. One route to market instead of five half built ones. A tighter definition of who the work was for. A sales process that did not rely on remembering to follow up. It was less impressive than the tactics I had been collecting, and it worked considerably better.

Since then I have done the same thing alongside agency, consultancy and coaching owners who were stuck in the identical loop. The work is not glamorous. It is picking one channel, saying no to the rest for a while, and holding the line long enough for it to compound. Owners who do that stop guessing what next quarter looks like.
