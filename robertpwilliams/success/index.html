Creative agency

A six person studio living entirely on referrals

Strong client work, an excellent reputation in one narrow network, and no idea where the next project was coming from once that network went quiet. The owner had tried outbound twice and abandoned it both times inside a month.

  • Rewrote the offer around the one project type that consistently made money, and priced it as a fixed engagement rather than a custom quote
  • Built a single outbound channel end to end, with the list, the message and the follow up sequence documented so a junior could run it
  • Put a weekly review in place so the process survived the first busy delivery period, which is where the previous two attempts died
2x
Average engagement value after repositioning
Independent consultancy

Busy every week, and still not making money

A solo consultant with a full calendar of small, fiddly engagements that ate the week and paid poorly. The acquisition problem was real, but it was downstream of a positioning problem nobody had named.

  • Narrowed the client definition to the segment already producing the best results, then rebuilt the offer for that segment specifically
  • Retired the low value work on a planned timeline rather than all at once, so revenue never dropped through the floor
  • Replaced ad hoc enquiries with a qualification call that filtered out poor fits before they reached a proposal
3 to 5
Qualified calls a week, holding steady
Coaching business

Plenty of audience, almost no conversations

Years of content, a decent following, and a sales process that amounted to hoping somebody would send a message. The traffic was never the issue. Nothing existed to turn attention into a booked call.

  • Built one clear route from content to a conversation, with a single call to action instead of five competing ones
  • Added a follow up rhythm for people who enquired and went quiet, which recovered a meaningful share of stalled conversations
  • Tightened the call itself so unsuitable prospects were filtered early and the right ones got a decision inside a week
