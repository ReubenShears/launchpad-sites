Questions

What owners ask before we start.

Who is this actually for?

Owners of agencies, consultancies and other online service businesses who are already delivering paid work and want that work to arrive predictably. If you have not sold anything yet, this is not the right fit and I will tell you on the first call.

Do you run the marketing for me?

No. I build the system with you and your team so it stays yours. That means the assets, the process and the judgement live inside your business rather than walking out of the door when an engagement ends.

How long does it take?

Twelve weeks is the standard shape. Positioning and offer land in the first fortnight, the acquisition channel is built and live inside the first month, and the rest of the time is spent running it and cutting what does not work.

Am I tied into a contract?

No long lock in. We agree the scope up front and work month to month within it. If it is not producing, you should be free to stop.

What do you need from me?

A few hours a week and a willingness to change the offer if the evidence says it needs changing. The owners who get the most out of this are the ones who make decisions quickly between sessions.
