What we do

Three ways we get an operation running clean.

Every engagement starts with what is actually happening on the floor, not what the org chart says should be happening.

◎

Operations diagnostic

We follow a real order, ticket or customer end to end and map every handoff, wait and rework loop. You get a clear picture of where time and margin are leaking, ranked by what is worth fixing first.

⌗

Process rebuild and documentation

We redesign the workflow around the outcome the customer is paying for, then write it up as SOPs, checklists and owner maps your team can follow on day one and train new hires with later.

◈

Systems and data integration

We connect the tools you already own so information moves once and stays consistent, then build the small set of reports leadership needs to see the operation without asking anyone for an update.

How it works

A structured engagement, not an open-ended retainer.

Diagnose

Two weeks of interviews, observation and data review. We map the current state honestly, including the workarounds people are too polite to mention, and agree what success will be measured on.

Rebuild

We design the future state with the people who have to live in it, pilot it on real work, and adjust until it holds under a normal week rather than a perfect one.

Hand over

Documentation, owner map, training and a simple scorecard so the improvement survives after we leave. If it only works while we are in the room, it did not work.

Questions

What people ask before we start.

How big does a company need to be to work with you?

Most of our clients are between ten and two hundred people, at the point where the founder can no longer see every order personally. Below that, the problem is usually still solvable with a conversation, and we will tell you so.

Do you replace our software?

Rarely. Most operations we see are running perfectly capable tools badly configured and poorly connected. We start by making what you own work properly, and only recommend a change when the tool genuinely cannot do the job.

Will this land as another consultant telling my team they are wrong?

No. The people doing the work already know where it breaks, they are just rarely asked. We design the new process with them, which is also the reason it survives after we leave.

How long is a typical engagement?

The diagnostic runs about two weeks. A full rebuild of one core process usually takes six to twelve weeks depending on how many teams it touches. Scope and fee are agreed up front, before any work starts.

What do we actually get at the end?

A documented process, a clear owner for every step, the integrations and reports built, and your team trained on all of it. Everything is yours to keep, edit and reuse.
