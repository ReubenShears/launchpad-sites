Our story

The problem is almost never the people doing the work.

Yemi spent years running order management and customer operations inside large equipment and healthcare organizations, the kind of environment where a single order passes through sales, engineering, logistics, service and finance before anyone gets paid. The pattern repeated everywhere: capable people, sensible intentions, and a process nobody had designed on purpose, held together by a handful of individuals who remembered how it all fit.

The fixes that worked were rarely dramatic. Map what really happens. Remove the handoffs that exist only because of an old org chart. Make the systems pass information once instead of three times. Write it down so it survives the next hire and the next quarter.

The Integrator Co. was started in 2023 to bring that discipline to smaller companies, the ones scaling fast enough to feel the strain but too lean to carry a full internal operations function. Engagements are principal-led, scoped up front, and finished with a handover rather than a dependency.

How we work

Three things we will not compromise on.

⌗

You keep everything

Process maps, SOPs, owner matrices, integrations and reporting are all yours, in your own systems, in editable form. If we never speak again, your operation still runs. We think that keeps us honest.

◈

We start on the floor

Before any recommendation we sit with the people doing the work and follow real orders through the system. Interviews with leadership tell you the intended process. The floor tells you the actual one.

◐

Scope agreed before we start

Fixed scope, fixed fee, a defined end. If the honest answer is that you need a hire rather than a consultant, or that the problem is smaller than the engagement, we will tell you before you spend anything.
