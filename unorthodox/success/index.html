Coaching

Cameron Easty needed volume he could actually handle.

Cameron could generate leads. What he could not do was work them properly at the volume he was creating, so the top of the funnel kept growing while the conversion rate quietly fell and good prospects sat unworked in a list.

We built an AI first qualification and nurture layer that touched every lead on arrival, sorted the ready from the not yet, and kept the rest warm automatically so nothing was left sitting.

  • 10,000 leads processed through the system
  • 500 conversions produced from that pool

Media & Community

Venture Room had a fast-growing audience and no route from it to revenue.

Jamal's audience was growing quickly, which looked like success and converted like a hobby. Attention was arriving daily with nowhere structured to go, so interest never became a conversation and conversations never became clients.

We built an AI powered funnel underneath the audience: a clear offer, a capture and qualification path, and automated follow up that turned passive followers into booked calls with the team.

  • Audience converted into tracked leads and revenue
  • Booked calls arriving from content rather than outbound effort
  • One system the team runs rather than a set of manual steps
