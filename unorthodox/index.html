We get asked

The questions that come up before people start.

I'm just getting started. Is this right for me?

We are best for businesses that are already moving: you know what you sell and who you sell it to, but the sales systems around it are manual and breaking. If you are earlier than that, book the call anyway and we will tell you honestly what to get right first.

How long does implementation take?

Timelines depend on how much is already in place, but most clients are live quickly and see results within four to six weeks of starting with us.

Is it fully automated, or does my team still work it?

The routine work is automated: qualification, follow up, booking and reminders run in the background. Your team keeps the parts that need a human, which is mostly the conversations that close deals.

Do you customize it for each business?

Yes. Every system is shaped around your sales process, your audience and your goals. We work one to one with clients, which is why we take on a limited number at a time.

What happens after setup?

We support the system so it keeps running properly, and offer ongoing consulting to help you adapt and optimize as volume grows. Everything is built to scale with you rather than be rebuilt at the next level.
