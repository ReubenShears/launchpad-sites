Questions

The things men ask before they start.

I travel constantly. Does that break the program?

No, it is one of the reasons the program is built the way it is. Sessions are 45 minutes and every one has a hotel gym version. Travel weeks get planned in advance rather than written off, which is the difference between a client who finishes 90 days and one who quietly stops at week five.

I have an old back or shoulder problem. Is this safe for me?

That is the normal starting point here, not the exception. The assessment exists specifically to find those issues and work around them. This is coaching, not medical treatment, so anything that needs a physician or a physical therapist gets referred out rather than trained through.

Do I need a gym?

A basic commercial gym or a reasonably equipped home setup is ideal. If you only have dumbbells and a bench, the program can be built around that. What matters far more than equipment is that you can get to it three times a week without a 40 minute drive attached.

How is this different from hiring a personal trainer?

A trainer counts your reps. The Rebuild starts with a proper assessment, treats the 90 days as one structured plan with defined checkpoints, and is designed around an executive schedule instead of assuming you have unlimited time. You get a coach who knows what your week actually looks like.

What does it cost?

The Rebuild is a fixed 90 day engagement, priced depending on session frequency and whether nutrition coaching is included. You get the exact number on the consult call, before you commit to anything, and there is no pressure to decide on the call itself.
