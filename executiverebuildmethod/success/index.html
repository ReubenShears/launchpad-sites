90 days
From daily back pain to none
Managing Partner · Age 47

Michael stopped planning his week around his lower back.

Michael had lived with a low grade back ache for four years. He had stopped deadlifting, stopped playing tennis, and had started standing up carefully in front of clients. Two previous trainers had him doing core work that made it worse, because nobody had looked at his hips.

The assessment found almost no hip extension and a spine doing the job his hips had quit. We spent the first three weeks restoring hip and thoracic range before loading anything, then rebuilt his hinge from the floor up.

  • Pain free through a normal working week by day 60
  • Back to deadlifting with a pattern that does not aggravate it
  • Playing tennis again for the first time in four years
3 a week
Held through two travel months
VP Sales · Age 44

David trained through the quarter that usually ends his training.

David was on a plane most weeks and had started every January for six years running. By March the program was always gone. He did not need more discipline, he needed a program that assumed the travel instead of pretending it away.

We built every session with a hotel gym version, planned the travel weeks a month ahead, and dropped the weekly target to three sessions so it was survivable rather than aspirational.

  • Held three sessions a week across his two heaviest travel months
  • Afternoon energy steady enough to move his hardest calls later in the day
  • First full quarter of consistent training in six years
Overhead
Shoulder back in full range
Founder · Age 52

Robert got his shoulder back and stopped training around it.

Robert had a shoulder that had not gone properly overhead since a rotator cuff issue in his forties. He had quietly removed every pressing movement from his training, and with them most of his upper body strength.

After clearing it with his physician we rebuilt scapular control and thoracic rotation first, reintroduced pressing at angles his shoulder tolerated, and worked back toward full overhead range over ten weeks.

  • Full pain free overhead range restored by day 90
  • Pressing back in the program for the first time in eight years
  • Lifting his grandson without thinking about the shoulder
