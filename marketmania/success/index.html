Covers booked outside opening hours
Restaurant

Neri's Kitchen stopped losing the tables it never heard about.

Lucia was running reservations from a paper book by the till. On a busy service nobody could get to the phone, and the callers who could not get through simply booked somewhere else. The obvious platforms wanted a cut of every cover and their own branding on the confirmation.

We put real time table availability on her own domain, set covers and turn times to match how the room actually runs, and added a deposit on tables of six or more.

  • More than a third of bookings now arrive when the restaurant is closed
  • Large table no shows down sharply once deposits went on
  • Every confirmation goes out branded as Neri's Kitchen

Missed appointments
Dental practice

Ridgeway Dental cut missed appointments by more than half.

Two receptionists were spending most of the morning on the phone confirming appointments and chasing recalls, and the practice was still losing chair time to patients who forgot. Every empty slot was a clinician sitting idle.

We loaded the full treatment list with the right appointment lengths per clinician, automated the recall reminders, and added text confirmations the day before and the morning of.

  • Missed appointments down 54% within three months
  • Reception time on the phone cut roughly in half
  • Recall bookings now fill themselves from the reminder
+27
Extra jobs booked per month
Plumbing & heating

Callow Plumbing started catching the evening callouts.

Most of the enquiries came in after six, when the engineers were driving home and nobody was answering. The jobs were there, they were just going to whoever picked up first.

We built a callout booking flow around two hour visit windows and engineer availability, with an automatic confirmation and a reminder the morning of the visit.

  • Twenty seven additional jobs booked in an average month
  • Most new bookings taken between 6pm and 10pm
  • Customers can now rebook without ringing the office
