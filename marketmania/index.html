Questions

The things people ask before they start.

What does white label actually mean here?

It means the system is yours in every way the customer can see. Your logo, your colours, your domain, your wording on every confirmation and reminder. Marketmania is the engine underneath and stays invisible.

Do you take a cut of my bookings?

No. You pay a flat monthly fee and keep every penny of what you take. Deposits and payments land in your own account, and we are never in the middle of that transaction.

Do I need a new website?

No. The booking system drops into the site you already have, whether that is a page we link to or a form embedded straight into it. If you do not have a site yet, we will host the booking pages on your domain.

Who owns the customer data?

You do. Every booking, contact detail and history record is yours, exportable at any time. If you ever leave, you take the list with you.

My business is not a restaurant or a dentist. Will it fit?

Very likely. If customers book time, a table, a treatment or a visit, the system handles it. We work with plumbers, roofers, landscapers, dentists, spas and restaurants, and the setup is shaped to the trade each time.
