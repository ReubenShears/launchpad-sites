Our story

It started with a diary, a pen and a lost Saturday night.

The first version of this system was built for one restaurant that was losing bookings every evening it got busy. The phone rang while the floor was full, nobody could reach it, and by the time anyone called back the table had gone elsewhere. The obvious answer was a booking platform, and the obvious answer came with a commission on every cover and a confirmation email branded as somebody else's business.

So we built something that did the job without the trade off. Real time availability, deposits, reminders, all sitting on the restaurant's own domain and going out under the restaurant's own name. Within a season the same system was running a dental practice's recall list and a plumber's callout diary, because underneath the language they all need the same thing: the right slot, the right person, confirmed and remembered.

That is what Marketmania is now. One booking and reservations engine, shaped to the trade in front of us, wearing the client's brand every single time.
