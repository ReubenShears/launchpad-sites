Questions

What people ask before they commit.

Is this business coaching or fitness coaching?

Both, and separating them is the mistake most people have already made. Your business will never outperform your body. We work the bottom line, the body and the mindset together, because pulling one lever while the other two drag is why the last attempt did not hold.

I am time strapped. Is this realistic for me?

Time strapped is the whole client profile. The plan is built around the week you actually have, which usually means fewer decisions, tighter standards and sessions you cannot talk yourself out of. If your week genuinely cannot hold it, I will tell you on the call.

What makes this different from what I have already tried?

You did not fail the last program because it was the wrong program. You failed it because nothing was in place to stop you renegotiating with yourself at 6am. I install the standards and the accountability first, then the tactics.

Do I have to be in Toronto?

No. I work with clients in person in Toronto and virtually everywhere else. Virtual clients get the same weekly contact, the same programming and the same directness.

Who is this not for?

Anyone looking for hand holding, motivational speeches or somewhere to outsource responsibility. If you want structure, standards and results we will work well together. If you want comfort, I am not your guy.
