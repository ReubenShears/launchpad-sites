Questions

What people ask before they start.

I have almost no time. Is this realistic for me?

Time strapped is the whole brief. The plan is built around the week you already have, which usually means shorter, harder sessions and food decisions made once rather than three times a day. If your week genuinely cannot hold anything, I will tell you that on the call.

Do I have to be in Toronto?

No. I coach in person in Toronto and virtually everywhere else. The virtual clients get the same weekly contact and the same programming, just over video.

Is this fitness coaching or business coaching?

Both, and that is deliberate. Some clients come for the body and end up fixing the pricing. Some come for the business and discover the energy problem was underneath it. We start wherever the pressure is highest.

What actually happens on the first call?

Thirty minutes. I ask about your health, your head and your numbers, and I tell you plainly what I would change first and in what order. You leave with a direction either way.

How long do people usually work with you?

We commit to ninety days first, because that is long enough to see a real change and short enough that neither of us can coast. Most clients carry on well past it, but that is a decision you make with the results in front of you.
