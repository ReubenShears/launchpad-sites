My story

Most people do not have a knowledge problem. They have a self-negotiation problem.

Thirty five years in, and I have watched the same thing happen in almost every room I have worked in. People arrive knowing exactly what to do. They have read the books, bought the programs, hired the consultant. And then they do not execute consistently. Not in the business, not with the body, not in how they think under pressure. The gap is never information. It is the quiet negotiation that happens at 6am, or at the point in the quarter where a standard gets renegotiated down and nobody notices.

So I stopped treating those as three separate problems. Your business will never outperform your body. Your results will never outperform your standards. I forensically examine, question and analyze the evidence from both your body and your business, then customize solutions that reframe the mindset, transform the body and increase the bottom line at the same time.

The work is direct because it has to be. There is no hand holding, no motivational speeches and no outsourcing responsibility. You are here to make an impact in people's lives, and that requires optimal performance from you first. I change your how to WOW.

The method

Three things I will not budge on.

◐

Non-negotiable standards

A standard you renegotiate is not a standard, it is a preference. We set the small number of things that hold regardless of the week, and then they hold regardless of the week.

◈

Identity-based execution

Motivation is a weather system and you cannot build on it. We change what you consider normal for someone like you, and the behaviour follows without needing to be talked into.

⌗

Body-first performance

Energy, confidence and leadership presence are physical advantages, not self-care. We fix the physical base first because everything else you are trying to do is running on it.
