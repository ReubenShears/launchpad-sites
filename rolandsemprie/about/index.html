My story

Thirty five years of watching people fix the wrong thing first.

I started where most trainers start, on the gym floor, writing programs for people who wanted to look and feel better. What I learned over the first decade is that the program is almost never the problem. People arrived with good plans and no capacity to keep them, and the reason was always somewhere else: a business bleeding their attention, a story they were telling themselves about who they were, a week with no room in it.

So I widened the work. Nutrition, because you cannot out train a diet built for someone else's life. Mindset, because the missed session and the undercharged invoice usually come from the same place. Then business, because for coaches, consultants and founders, the health problem and the profit problem are the same problem wearing different clothes.

That became the Results Regardless approach: whatever is going on, whatever the schedule looks like, we find the version of the plan that survives your actual week. It is the reason I still take on a limited number of people and coach every one of them myself.

How I work

Three things I will not budge on.

◐

The truth first, always

If your week cannot hold the plan, I will say so before you pay me. If the business problem is bigger than the body problem, we start there instead. Comfortable coaching is expensive coaching.

◈

One plan, not three

You should not be running a training plan, a nutrition plan and a business plan that argue with each other. Everything I build is one plan with one set of priorities and one place to look each week.

⌗

Results regardless

Travel weeks, hard quarters, sick kids, blown deadlines. Life is not a reason the plan stops, it is the thing the plan has to be built for. We adjust weekly and keep moving.
