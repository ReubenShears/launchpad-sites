Founder · Toronto

A founder who had not trained properly in four years.

Marcus was running a growing agency on five hours of sleep, coffee and whatever was closest at lunch. He had bought three programs in two years and finished none of them, and had decided the problem was discipline.

It was not. We cut the training to three short sessions he could not talk himself out of, built a food plan around client lunches instead of pretending they would not happen, and spent the first fortnight on the story he was telling himself about being too busy.

  • Training four mornings a week by the end of the first quarter
  • Sleeping seven hours on a normal week for the first time in years
  • Stopped taking the calls that were costing him the mornings

Rate increase, same client load
Coach & consultant · Virtual

A coach who was fully booked and barely paid.

Deanna had a full roster and no room left in the week. Every solution she had been sold involved finding more clients, which was the one thing her calendar could not absorb.

We left the client count alone and worked on the offer instead: what she was actually delivering, what it was worth, and why she had been pricing it as though it were not. Then we rebuilt her week so the coaching hours sat where her energy was highest.

  • Rates doubled across the roster over two renewal cycles
  • Same number of clients, four fewer working hours a week
  • First quarter in three years she did not consider quitting
18 mo
And still holding the changes
Executive · Toronto

An executive who had run out of energy before she ran out of ambition.

Ray came in for weight loss and left with something bigger. The weight was real, but the thing underneath it was a decade of putting herself last on a list she had written herself.

We started with the mindset work because nothing else would have held, then layered in strength training and a nutrition approach built for travel weeks. The plan changed shape at least six times in the first ninety days, which is the point.

  • Down two dress sizes and holding it a year and a half later
  • Strength training three times a week, including on the road
  • Took the promotion she had turned down twice before
