18 kg
Lost across ten months, still off
Fat loss · Shift worker

Rachel stopped losing the same fifteen kilos over and over.

Rachel had done three separate diets in five years, each one working until her roster changed. Night shifts wrecked her eating, the plan had no version of a night shift in it, and every attempt ended the same way.

We rebuilt her week around the roster instead of ignoring it: two strength sessions on days off, a simple eating framework with a specific night shift version, and a short list of habits to lock in before anything else was added.

  • 18 kg down across ten months, at a pace she barely noticed
  • Still within two kilos of goal weight eight months after finishing
  • Trains three times a week now without it feeling like a project

Sessions a week, kept up for a year
Strength · Parent of two

Marcus went from no training at all to four sessions a week he does not skip.

Marcus had a gym membership he used twice a month and a lot of guilt about it. Between work and two young kids, every programme he had tried assumed a free hour he simply did not have.

We started with two thirty minute home sessions, built the habit for six weeks before touching nutrition, then added gym sessions once the first two had become automatic rather than negotiable.

  • Trains four times a week and has done for over a year
  • Waist down 11 cm while getting noticeably stronger
  • Sleeping better and off the afternoon energy drinks
12 kg
Lost without cutting out a single food
Nutrition · Frequent traveller

Priya lost weight while eating out four nights a week.

Priya travelled constantly for work and had been told by two previous coaches to prep her meals in advance. That advice was useless to somebody living out of hotels, so she assumed the problem was her.

Instead of a meal plan we built a set of ordering rules that work in almost any restaurant, a hotel room training template needing no equipment, and a weekly check in timed for her Sunday travel day.

  • 12 kg lost across seven months with no food off limits
  • Kept the result through a six week work trip
  • Now maintains without tracking anything day to day
