Questions

The things people ask before they start.

Do I need a gym membership?

No. Plenty of clients train at home with dumbbells or bands, and some with nothing at all to begin with. Tell us what you have access to and the programme is written around it.

Will you put me on a strict meal plan?

No. Strict meal plans are exactly why the weight comes back. You get targets, a framework and a short list of swaps, then we build your normal meals into it, including takeaways and time out with friends.

How much time does this take each week?

Most clients train three to four times a week, for around forty five minutes. If your calendar only allows two sessions, we plan for two rather than setting you up to miss a third.

I have tried coaching before and it did not work. Why is this different?

Most coaching sells a twelve week plan and disappears. Here the plan changes every week based on what your life actually did, and the goal is that you no longer need us at the end of it.

What if it is not right for me?

Then you take the 30 day money back guarantee and we part on good terms. We would rather refund one person than drag somebody through a programme they are not suited to.
