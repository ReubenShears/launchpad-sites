Our story

FST started because the fitness industry keeps selling the same thirty days.

Almost everyone who comes to FST arrives with the same story. They have done the eight week shred, the shake plan, the app that sent the same workout to ten thousand people. It worked, briefly. Then a busy month arrived, the plan had no room in it for a busy month, and everything went back to where it started plus a bit.

That is not a discipline problem, it is a design problem. A plan that only works on a perfect week is guaranteed to fail, because nobody gets many perfect weeks. So FST was built the other way round: start from the week you actually have, make the smallest set of changes that will move the needle, and adjust it as your life changes rather than pretending it will not.

That means fewer clients, taken on properly, coached one to one. It also means we would rather refund somebody in the first month than keep them on a programme that is not right for them, which is why the 30 day money back guarantee exists.

How we work

Three things we will not budge on.

⌗

Your plan is yours

Nothing is copied and pasted. Your training, your targets and your habits are written for your schedule, your equipment and your history, then rewritten as those things change.

◈

No food is banned

Cutting out everything you enjoy is why the weight comes back. We build the meals out with you and around you, so the plan still works on a birthday, a holiday or a Friday night.

◐

We say when it is not a fit

If you need a physiotherapist, a doctor or simply a different approach, you will hear that on the first call rather than after you have paid. Honest beats hopeful every time.
