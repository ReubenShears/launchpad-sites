Questions

The things operators ask before they start.

How much should I be spending?

Most operators we work with start between $1,000 and $3,000 a month in ad spend, depending on the size of the market and the services they are pushing. We will tell you honestly on the first call if your budget is not going to move the needle yet.

Am I locked into a contract?

No. We work month to month. If the work is not producing reservations you should be free to walk, and enough operators stay with us for years that we do not need to lock anybody in.

Who owns the ad account?

You do, always. We build inside your own Google Ads account, so if we ever part ways the account, the data and the entire history stay with you.

How quickly will I see inquiries?

Campaigns typically go live within seven working days and most operators see their first calls inside the opening fortnight. The real gains land between months two and four as we strip out the waste.

Do you work with party buses and black car only fleets?

Yes to both. Sedans, SUVs, stretch limos, sprinters and party buses all get their own campaign structure, because the person booking a prom limo and the person booking a Monday airport run search in completely different ways.
