Our story

We got tired of watching good operators pay for bad clicks.

Goeasymedia started the way most useful things do, out of frustration. We were managing paid search for a handful of local service businesses when a limousine operator came to us holding twelve months of statements from a national agency. The spend was immaculate. The reporting was beautiful. Nobody could tell him how many cars had actually left the lot because of it.

So we rebuilt it from the ground up. We tracked every call, tied it back to the search that produced it, and threw away everything that produced nothing. Within a quarter his cost per booked ride had roughly halved and his midweek corporate work, the stuff that keeps a fleet profitable, was finally moving. He sent us two other operators the following month, and that was that.

Since then we have worked with dozens of limousine, black car and party bus companies across the country, and we have deliberately stayed narrow. Every account we run teaches us something we can use in the next one: which negative keywords save real money, how prom season really behaves, why a wedding inquiry needs a different landing page than an airport run. That accumulated knowledge is the entire product. You are not paying for a media buyer who learned your industry on your budget.
