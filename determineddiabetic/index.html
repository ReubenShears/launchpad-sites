Questions

What people ask before they start.

Is the DD-Diary really free?

Yes. Creating your account and using the diary costs nothing. Log your day, build your charts and earn your Determination Score without paying for anything. Coaching is the paid part, and it is entirely your choice whether you ever add it.

Is this for Type 1 or Type 2?

Both, and prediabetes too. The tools are the same because the daily work is the same: see what is happening, understand why, and change one thing at a time. Your coach tailors the plan to your type, your medication and your routine.

What happens on the free strategy session?

We look at where you are now, what is affecting your blood sugar day to day, and the habits getting in the way. You leave with a clear next step and an honest view of whether coaching would help you. There is no pressure to continue.

Does this replace my doctor?

No, and it never should. Determined Diabetic provides coaching, education and accountability, not medical advice. We work alongside your care team, and we will always tell you when a question belongs with them instead of us.

How much time does it take?

Logging takes a couple of minutes a day, less once it becomes routine. Coaching is a weekly session plus whatever you choose to work on between them. The whole point is a system that fits your life, not another job on top of it.
