About us

We are determined diabetics.

Determined Diabetic was not built in a boardroom. It was built by people who have lived the frustration, the loneliness and the small daily wins of managing diabetes, and who believed the whole experience could be better.

Our story

It started with a diary that nobody had built yet.

Grant Weiss has lived with Type 1 diabetes for more than 25 years. Like most people with the condition, he spent years doing everything he was told to do and still felt like he was guessing. The information existed, scattered across meters, apps, notes and memory, but nothing pulled it together into something a person could actually act on.

So he built the tool he wished he had been handed at diagnosis. The Determined Diabetic Diary makes a day visible: glucose, food, movement, hydration, medication, sleep, all in one place, all rolled into a single daily Determination Score. Suddenly the patterns were not hidden anymore. They were on the screen.

Then came the part the app alone could never solve. Diabetes is lonely, and data does not hug you back. That is why the diary grew into a full program: expert coaching, education, rewards and a team that keeps showing up on the weeks when your own motivation does not. We believe we can, and should, do better for everyone living with diabetes. This is our attempt at exactly that.

How we work

Three things we will not compromise on.

◐

Your plan, not a template

Two people with the same diagnosis can need completely different routines. We start from your data, your schedule and your food, and build a system around the life you actually have.

◇

Honest, never preachy

Nobody with diabetes needs another lecture about willpower. We tell you the truth about what the numbers show, then help you change one thing at a time without shame attached.

✦

Progress gets celebrated

Streaks, badges, points and prizes exist for a reason. When the work is daily and lifelong, marking the wins is not a gimmick. It is what keeps people going.
