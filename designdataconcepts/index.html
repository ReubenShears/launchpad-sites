Questions

The things people ask before they start.

Do I have to move my hosting to you?

No, but most clients do. Managed hosting is how we keep updates, security, backups and content changes moving without a support ticket every time something needs doing. If you would rather stay where you are, we will work with your current setup.

Which platform will my site be built on?

Whichever one fits the project. We work across WordPress, Webflow, WebinOne, Wix, Shopify and others, and we will tell you which we recommend and why before any work starts.

How long does a rebuild take?

A straightforward marketing site typically runs six to ten weeks from kickoff. Ecommerce and multi-location projects take longer. The biggest variable is how quickly content and photography come together, and we help with both.

What does ongoing maintenance actually include?

Core and plugin updates, security monitoring, scheduled backups, uptime checks, SEO housekeeping and routine content edits. In plain terms, you email us a change and it gets done, and the things you would never think to check get checked anyway.

Do you only work with local businesses?

We are based in Charlottesville, Virginia and a good number of our clients are local, but we manage sites for companies from Delaware and Pennsylvania to New Jersey and beyond. The work is the same wherever you are.
