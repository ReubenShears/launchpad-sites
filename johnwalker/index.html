Questions

The things people ask before they start.

What exactly does a digital marketing consultation include?

A consultation typically covers a full review of your current marketing efforts across website, social, email, ads and SEO. It identifies gaps or missed opportunities and gives you a clear action plan. Think of it as a strategy session to help you prioritize what actually drives growth.

How much does digital marketing cost for a small business?

It depends on your goals and budget. Some businesses start with a few hundred dollars per month for essentials like website optimization and email marketing. Others invest more for ads and full-service campaigns. A good consultant tailors a plan that gives you the best return, not a one-size-fits-all package.

How long will it take to see results?

Quick wins such as improved website conversions or better ad targeting can happen in weeks, but sustainable growth typically builds over three to six months. Digital marketing is a mix of strategy and momentum. The more consistent the execution, the faster results compound.

Do I need to manage anything myself, or do you handle everything?

You can be as hands-on or hands-off as you want. Some business owners prefer me to run everything from content to campaigns, while others stay involved in approvals and messaging. The goal is to make marketing easy and effective without overwhelming your time.

What if I do not have a big marketing team, or any at all?

That is actually where I shine. Most small businesses do not have in-house marketing teams, so I act as your on-demand marketing department, bringing strategy, tools and execution together so you can focus on running your business.
