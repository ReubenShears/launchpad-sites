My story

Most small businesses do not need an agency. They need one person who knows what they are doing.

I have spent more than fifteen years around digital marketing, as a marketer and as an entrepreneur running my own ventures. That second part matters more than it sounds. When you have carried the cost of a campaign that did not work on your own books, you stop being casual about where a client's money goes.

What I kept seeing was the same pattern. A small business owner signs up with an agency, gets handed a package built for somebody else, and six months later has a folder of reports full of impressions and no clearer idea whether any of it produced a customer. The work was not bad exactly. It just was not built for them, and nobody was accountable for the outcome.

So I built the opposite. You get one consultant who reviews what you already have, tells you honestly what is worth fixing first, and then does the work. Website, email, ads and automation under one roof, priced to your budget rather than to a tier. Over a hundred small businesses later, that is still how it runs.

How I work

Three things I will not budge on.

⌗

You own everything

The website, the ad accounts, the email list and the automations are yours and stay yours. If we ever part ways, you keep the assets and the history. I think that keeps me honest.

◈

Plain English, always

No acronyms and no vanity metrics. Your update says what came in, what it cost and what I changed. If a report needs a glossary, I have written it badly.

◐

Honest about fit

If your budget is not ready to compete, or marketing is not the bottleneck yet, I will say so on the first call and point you at what will help instead. A client I cannot get results for costs me more than they pay.
