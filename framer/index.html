Questions

The things people ask before they start.

Do I need to know how to code?

No. Everything on the canvas is visual, and the site it publishes is production ready. If you do write code, you can add your own components and effects whenever you want more control.

Can I use my own domain?

Yes. Point a domain you already own at your site, or start on a free Framer subdomain while you build and connect the real one when you are ready to launch.

What happens to my content if I leave?

It stays yours. Your CMS content can be exported, and nothing about your copy, images or brand is locked to us in a way you cannot take with you.

Will my site actually be fast?

Sites publish to a global network with images, fonts and scripts optimized automatically, which is why Core Web Vitals on a typical Framer site land comfortably in the good range.

Can a whole team work on the same site?

Yes. Multiple people can edit together in real time, and branches let bigger changes be built and reviewed before anything reaches the live site.
