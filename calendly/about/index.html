About us

We took the friction out of finding time.

Our story

It started with one very ordinary frustration.

Before Calendly existed, booking a meeting meant a chain of emails that could run for days. Someone proposed three times, someone else rejected all three, a fourth was suggested and by then the reason for meeting had cooled off. Every business ran on this, and everybody accepted it as the cost of doing business.

Tope Awotona did not accept it. He put his own savings into building a scheduling tool that simply removed the negotiation: publish when you are free, share one link, and let the other person choose. The first version launched in 2013 out of Atlanta with a small team and no outside funding.

What followed was word of mouth rather than advertising. Salespeople sent the link to prospects, recruiters sent it to candidates, professors sent it to students, and each of those people saw how much easier it was and wanted one of their own. Today Calendly is used inside 86 percent of the Fortune 500 as well as by hundreds of thousands of individuals, and the product has grown into workflows, routing, team scheduling and enterprise administration. The founding idea has not changed at all.
