Questions

The things people ask before they start.

Is there really a free plan?

Yes. The free plan covers one event type, unlimited meetings and a connected calendar, with no credit card needed to start. Standard and Teams plans add multiple event types, workflows, routing and admin controls when you need them.

Which calendars and conferencing tools does it work with?

Google, Outlook, Office 365 and Exchange calendars, plus Zoom, Microsoft Teams, Google Meet and Webex for the meeting itself. Over 100 integrations connect Calendly to the CRM, payment and marketing tools you already run.

How does it actually reduce no-shows?

Workflows send a text reminder before the meeting starts and a follow-up email after it ends, automatically, on every booking. Invitees can reschedule from the confirmation rather than quietly disappearing.

Can a whole team share availability?

Yes. Round robin rotates meetings across the team so nobody is overloaded, and collective events combine several people's calendars to find a slot that suits everyone. Admins can standardize event types across the organization.

Is my scheduling data secure?

Calendly is built for enterprise use, with SOC 2 and ISO 27001 certification, GDPR and CCPA compliance, PCI DSS handling for payments, and admin controls covering single sign-on, data governance and audit.
